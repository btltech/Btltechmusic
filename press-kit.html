Artist & Founder EPK

BTLTECH Music

BTLTECH Music is a UK-based independent music and music-technology project blending Afrobeats, Afro-fusion, Afro-soul, lovers rock, and Lagos/London storytelling. Shaped between Nigerian roots and London creativity, BTLTECH releases independent records alongside developing creator tools like Lyrid and producing the Choko King world.

Recent flagship BTLTECH releases include "You Lie" (Official Lyric Video, 16 Nov 2026), "Hold My Waist" (with VAELMIRAH) (15 Nov 2026), "Stacking the Cash" (12 Nov 2026), "Yahoo Man" (9 Nov 2026), "Weak Knees" (30 Oct 2026), "Paycheck to Paycheck" (2 Oct 2026), "No Photocopy" (18 Sept 2026), and "NO CONTEST" (31 July 2026) — alongside the 4-hour BTLTECH Music Radio stream on YouTube.

Virtual Artist Bio

Choko King

Choko King is a virtual Afro-dancehall artist — a faceless, crown-wearing ragga toaster carrying Lagos heat into the London cold. Sweet name, dangerous flavour: deep gravel vocals, Nigerian pidgin laced with Jamaican patois, and street anthems built for the dancefloor. Created by BTLTECH Music, his catalogue includes "Sweet Fire," "London Outside," and latest single "Go Slow."

Choko King is the sound of two cities at once — the heat, hustle and noise of Lagos pressed against the grey cold of London. A faceless figure in a gold crown, he toasts in a deep, raw gravel voice that slides between Nigerian pidgin and Jamaican patois, turning street life into anthems. Sweet name, dangerous flavour.

His music is Afro-dancehall at its core — ragga toasting, Afrobeats bounce and dancehall riddims — moving from sweaty club bangers to conscious roots reflections without ever losing the swagger. Whether it is turn-up energy for the dancefloor or a hard truth about the hustle, every track carries the same signature: rugged, melodic, and unmistakably him.

Choko King is a virtual artist, created and produced by BTLTECH Music. Like Gorillaz or Hatsune Miku before him, he is a character brought to life — a persona with a world, a voice and a story, built openly rather than hidden. The crown stays on; the face stays off. The focus is the music and the myth.

Following debut single "Sweet Fire," his growing catalogue now includes "London Outside" and latest single "Go Slow." Lagos-born in spirit, London-cold in delivery, crown never melting: Choko King is building a world one record at a time.
