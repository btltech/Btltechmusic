About Choko King

The Crown Stays On

Choko King is the sound of two cities at once — the heat, hustle and noise of Lagos pressed against the grey cold of London. A faceless figure in a gold crown, he toasts in a deep, raw gravel voice that slides between Nigerian pidgin and Jamaican patois, turning street life into anthems. Sweet name, dangerous flavour.

His music is Afro-dancehall at its core — ragga toasting, Afrobeats bounce and dancehall riddims — moving from sweaty club bangers to conscious roots reflections without ever losing the swagger. Whether it is turn-up energy for the dancefloor or a hard truth about the hustle, every track carries the same signature: rugged, melodic, and unmistakably him.

Choko King is a virtual artist, created and produced by BTLTECH Music. Like Gorillaz or Hatsune Miku before him, he is a character brought to life — a persona with a world, a voice and a story, built openly rather than hidden. The songs are written and directed by BTLTECH; the vocals are AI-generated — a human-led, AI-powered artist, disclosed openly rather than hidden. The crown stays on; the face stays off. The focus is the music and the myth.

After opening with his debut single "Sweet Fire," Choko King expands the catalogue with "London Outside" and "Go Slow." Lagos-born in spirit, London-cold in delivery, crown never melting: Choko King is building a world one record at a time.
