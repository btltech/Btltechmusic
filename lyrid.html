Features

Everything you need to ship a lyric video

From raw track to polished vertical clip in minutes.

🎯

Automatic lyric alignment

Lyrid separates the vocal, aligns your supplied lyrics with the performance, and builds word-level timing automatically. Fine-tune the result when needed.

🗣️

Your lyrics, exactly as written

Lyrid preserves the wording, names, spelling, slang, and creative phrasing you provide while matching it to the performance.

📊

Song Read — understand your arrangement

Analyse the structure of your track before you create. Song Read measures sections, energy, repetition, hook timing and loudness changes to help you understand how your arrangement develops. It describes measurable characteristics of the recording — it does not score musical quality or predict commercial success.

🎨

42 Looks & Raw Media

Choose from 42 Looks, use Raw Media for untouched footage, or start quickly with 8 curated shortcuts. Auto-Design, motion, glow and beat-reactive options give you plenty of room to make the result your own.

✂️

Social clips or full-length videos

Cut any hook into a vertical clip for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, with smooth audio fades. Or export the full song.

🎬

Multi-clip video & Lyrics Off

Import multiple video clips on a filmstrip with hard cuts, or turn lyrics off in Design → Background to export a plain music video.

🔒

Private by design

Audio processing stays on your Mac. Your music files are never uploaded for audio processing. Network access is only used for setup, licensing and optional services described in our Privacy Policy.

📐

Formats for every major platform

Export in 9:16, 1:1, 16:9 or 4:5, in MP4 or MOV, with a one-click Publish Pack for multiple platforms.

✦

Halo ✦ section motion

Halo ✦ follows your song's structure. Add [Verse] and [Hook] markers to your lyrics and it holds back through verses, then opens up on the chorus. Needs Smart motion above zero.

⚡

Beat-reactive glitch effects

Beat-gated RGB split, CRT scanlines and slice jitter that fire on the beat — not constantly. Includes the free Glitch Pop preset.

♻️

Brand Kits & custom styles

Manage custom styles in the Brand tab. Duplicate a project keeping its sync, restyle and re-export in minutes, or share looks as .lyridstyle files.

FAQ

Lyrid questions answered

What is Lyrid?

Lyrid is a Mac app that automatically aligns your lyrics to the performance and builds polished lyric videos for social platforms.

Can I use my own spelling and custom lyrics?

Yes. Lyrid uses the exact lyrics you provide, preserving your wording, spelling, and phrasing. You can also generate an editable transcription draft when you do not already have the lyrics.

Do I need video-editing experience?

No. Lyrid aligns the lyrics for you and gives you 42 ready-made Looks — you pick a style and export.

Which platforms can I make videos for?

Export videos sized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, YouTube and Spotify Canvas, with vertical, square, widescreen and portrait sizes.

Does my music get uploaded anywhere?

Audio processing stays on your Mac. Your music files are never uploaded for audio processing. Network access is only used for setup, licensing and optional services described in our Privacy Policy.
