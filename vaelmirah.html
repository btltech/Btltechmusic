About VAELMIRAH

Bold energy. Soft melodies. Unforgettable rhythms.

VAELMIRAH brings a fresh feminine voice to the BTLTECH Music universe, blending late-night Afro-R&B intimacy, soulful melodic runs, and infectious Afrobeats rhythms.

Set against the glow of nighttime cityscapes and shimmering waterfront reflections, VAELMIRAH operates with a nocturnal mystique — a faceless silhouette adorned with a crescent moon pendant, singing of distance, late-night longing, city rain, and connections that hit different after dark.

Her debut single Moon Low showcases her sound: warm, velvety vocal textures wrapped in warm synths, crisp percussion, and deep basslines crafted for headphone intimacy and late-night drives.
