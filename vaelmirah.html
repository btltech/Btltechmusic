About VAELMIRAH

Bold energy. Soft melodies. Unforgettable rhythms.

VAELMIRAH brings a fresh feminine voice to the BTLTECH Music universe, blending late-night Afro-R&B intimacy, soulful melodic runs, and infectious Afrobeats rhythms.

Set against the glow of nighttime cityscapes and shimmering waterfront reflections, VAELMIRAH operates with a nocturnal mystique — singing of resilience, self-made peace, distance across Lagos rain and London cold, and connections that hit different after dark.

Following her atmospheric debut single Moon Low (16 October 2026) and her empowering follow-up anthem Soft Life (30 October 2026), VAELMIRAH's 6-track debut EP HER OWN WORLD is out now (released 15 November 2026), featuring collaborations with BTLTECH (Hold My Waist) and Choko King (Bad for Me) alongside Boss Mode and Jo (The Whisper Dance).
