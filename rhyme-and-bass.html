About RHYME & BASS

Old rhymes. New rhythms. Growing up in bass.

RHYME & BASS is a faceless music collective reimagining familiar childhood melodies through modern Black British and African production. UK Drill, Jersey Club, Afrobeats, UK Funky and Afroswing become the foundation for songs about resilience, growth, community, confidence and finding your own path.

Rooted in the warmth and energy of urban UK playgrounds, RHYME & BASS captures a slightly nostalgic yet undeniably modern sound. By pairing memory-evoking melodic structures with heavy bass and contemporary club riddims, the collective bridges generations and sounds.

Identity

The Collective

Four faceless members bound by sound and color set against an urban UK playground.

RHYME & BASS functions as a unified faceless group rather than fixed real-world performers. Different vocalists lead different records while the collective visual and sonic identity remains consistent across every release.

MEMBER 01 Pink

Faceless Female

Recognisable by her pink outerwear and hoodie. Brings vibrant energy and sharp melodic leads to the collective.

MEMBER 02 Green

Faceless Male

Identified by his olive green parka and straps. Grounded basslines, rhythmic bounce, and raw delivery.

MEMBER 03 Cream / Yellow

Faceless Female

Defined by her cream hoodie, bucket hat, and yellow accents. Warm, soulful hooks and nostalgic bounce.

MEMBER 04 Black / Purple

Faceless Male

Marked by deep black layers and purple details. Heavy drill cadence, atmospheric tones, and street wisdom.
